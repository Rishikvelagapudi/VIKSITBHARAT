# 🇮🇳 VIKSIT BHARAT @ 2047 | The Sovereign Blueprint

> **"A Developed India by 2047: 1.4 Billion Citizens, One Resolute Destiny."**

An Indian Tricolor-themed, futuristic landing page and sovereign portal commemorating the vision of **Viksit Bharat @ 2047** (Developed India by the Centenary of Independence).

Built taking the cybernetic, command-center UI/UX of **VIKSIT ANDHRA** as direct inspiration, completely transformed into an authentic **Tricolor (Deep Saffron #FF671F, Pure White #FFFFFF, Ashoka Chakra Navy #000080, and India Emerald Green #10B981)** aesthetic.

- Diagonal sovereign laser light streak across the viewport.
- **FX ON/OFF** toggle button in header for low-power devices.

- Zero external audio assets required.
- High-fidelity real-time sound synthesis:

  - **Audio ON/OFF** mute toggle button with state persistence.
